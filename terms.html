Editor de Lives is a desktop tool that edits short clips from Twitch livestreams and sends them to the user's own TikTok account, as a draft or as a post, only after the user reviews and approves each clip.

You are responsible for having the right to publish each clip (for example, the streamer's permission or a clipping program), and for following TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and intellectual property rules. Clips will be removed if a rights holder asks.

You connect your TikTok account through TikTok's official login (Login Kit). You can disconnect at any time in the app or in TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions).
