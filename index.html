Editor de Lives

Editor de Lives is a desktop tool that turns highlights from Twitch livestreams into short vertical clips (crop, captions, hook text) and sends them to the owner's TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Every clip credits the original streamer (twitch.tv/<channel>) in the post text. The user connects their TikTok account with Login Kit, reviews each clip, chooses the privacy level, and then the clip is uploaded as a draft or posted to their own profile.
