Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. What data we access

When you connect your TikTok account, the app receives your basic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar) and an access token, with the permissions you approve (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).

2. How we use it

This data is used only to show which account is connected, to show your posting options (privacy levels, interaction settings) and to upload the videos you approve to your own TikTok account.

3. Where it is stored

All data, including the access token and the videos, stays only on your own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API. We do not sell or share data with anyone.

4. Deleting your data

You can disconnect your account in the app at any time, which deletes the stored token. You can also revoke access in TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions).

5. Contact

For questions or removal requests, message the TikTok profile where the videos are posted.
